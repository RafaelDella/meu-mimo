# Assets do protótipo

## Sticker principal

- Arquivo: `dist/assets/mimo-kitten.png`.
- Gerado com a ferramenta integrada ImageGen, com fundo transparente.
- Uso: colagem principal no cartão de saldo, com gatinho, acessórios e contorno de sticker.

Prompt final:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent cutout scrapbook sticker for a premium feminine mobile app named Meu Mimo. Create one adorable photographic fluffy cream-and-white kitten seen chest up, looking straight at camera, wearing oversized translucent blush-pink heart-shaped sunglasses and a small satin pink bow on its head. Whimsical fashion magazine collage, real soft fur texture, a slightly cheeky lovable expression, delicate adult coquette aesthetic. The kitten holds a tiny pink love envelope with its front paws. A clean thick off-white die-cut sticker outline around the subject. Add only two tiny isolated pink sparkle stickers next to the kitten. Composition: centered compact silhouette, all ears, paws and accessories entirely inside frame, comfortably padded. Colors: pastel pink #F2A7C4, blush #FCE8F0, white #FFFFFF. Soft natural studio light. Genuine transparent background, no background rectangle, no lettering, no watermark, no UI.
